Passer rating is calculated from completions, attempts, passing yards, passing touchdowns and interceptions when those fields are available. Added advanced metrics include passing efficiency, rushing/receiving efficiency, catch rate, total offensive yards/touchdowns, tackles, defensive impact plays and standard fantasy points (0.5 PPR). Derived stats require the relevant source fields; unavailable data is shown as a dash rather than treated as a real zero.
Data source: nflverse-data releases. Independent fan-made project, not affiliated with the NFL. Data availability depends on the CSV files published in the repository. Use Upload CSV to inspect a compatible player-stat dataset.
