How this backtest works

  • Games are processed in date order, separately by season.
  • Only completed games earlier than the game being predicted are used to calculate each team's record, scoring average, recent form and opponent win percentage.
  • Early-season estimates are pulled toward a league-average baseline to reduce overreaction to tiny samples.
  • Probabilities are intentionally conservative and are not betting odds.
  • Injuries, weather, rest and travel are not included in this historical test yet, so the score is a baseline to improve on.
