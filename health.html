What these checks mean

The build checks that the expected source files exist, are non-empty and have recognisable headers; the records and player indexes are valid JSON and contain season metadata; and the published compressed play-by-play files are present. These checks cannot prove every statistic is correct or that every upstream source is error-free. Review metric definitions and coverage in the Research Lab.
