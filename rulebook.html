Start here: how an NFL game works

The offence tries to move the ball downfield and score. The defence tries to stop it, force a turnover or make the offence give the ball away. Teams usually have four downs to gain 10 yards; reaching the line to gain earns a new set of downs.

Scoring

Touchdown: 6 points. Extra-point kick: 1. Successful try from scrimmage: 2. Field goal: 3. Safety: 2.

Possession

Possession can change after a punt, kickoff, turnover, missed field goal, score or failure to gain a first down.

Clock

The game has four 15-minute quarters. Clock rules vary by play, stoppage, timeout and late-game situation.

Flags

A penalty is enforced according to the foul, its location, the down and special rules. The flag colour alone does not tell you the full outcome.
