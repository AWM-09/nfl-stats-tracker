Data Quality Centre

COVERAGE · FRESHNESS

Audits the published player-week files. This describes the site's source files; it is not a certification against official record books.

Audit is on demand, so opening this page does not download every historical CSV.
How coverage is calculated

For each selected season, the report counts rows and grouped player-game events, checks whether required source fields exist, and measures populated values. Missing fields are unavailable, not zero. Coverage does not itself verify every official box score.

The published weekly files do not provide authoritative draft position or rookie-class data. This tool reports the first season a player appears in your selected files; it must not be treated as a confirmed rookie year.
